ProfileSmith

原生 macOS 描述文件管理器。自动索引本机所有 .mobileprovision / .provisionprofile，一眼看清哪些快过期、属于哪个 Team、绑定了哪些证书和 Entitlements。 A native provisioning profile manager for macOS. It indexes every .mobileprovision and .provisionprofile on your Mac and shows at a glance which ones expire soon, which Team they belong to, and which certificates and entitlements they carry.

安装Install

不确定芯片类型？打开「苹果菜单 › 关于本机」，看“芯片”或“处理器”一栏，或在终端执行 uname -m。Not sure which chip you have? Open › About This Mac and check “Chip” or “Processor”, or run uname -m in Terminal.

  1. 下载与本机芯片匹配的 DMG：Apple 芯片选 arm64，Intel 芯片选 x86_64。Download the DMG for your chip: arm64 for Apple silicon, x86_64 for Intel.
  2. 打开 DMG，把 ProfileSmith 拖进「应用程序」文件夹，再从「应用程序」中打开。Open the DMG, drag ProfileSmith into Applications and launch it from there.
  3. ProfileSmith 会自动索引本机的描述文件；也可以把描述文件或 IPA 直接拖进窗口导入、预览。ProfileSmith indexes the profiles on your Mac automatically. You can also drag profiles or IPAs into the window to import or preview them.
  4. 之后的新版本由内置的 Sparkle 自动更新，会按本机芯片下载对应的安装包。New versions arrive through the built-in Sparkle updater, which downloads the package for your chip.
